How to Install (Sideload)

Since these apps are hosted outside the Google Play Store, you need to grant your device permission to install them.

  • 1 Download the .apk file by clicking "Download" on any app.
  • 2 Open the downloaded file. Your phone will show a warning about Unknown Sources.
  • 3 Click Settings on the prompt and toggle on "Allow from this source".
  • 4 Go back and click Install. You're all set!

Play Protect Warning

Google Play Protect might flag these apps because they are self-signed. You can safely tap "More details" -> "Install anyway".
