WELCOME TO MY WEBPAGE

Hello, It's me Hem Chand, from Kailali , Nepal. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Agriculture at Far Western University, School of Agriculture, Tikapur. I have a strong interest in agriculture, information technology, and social studies.

I am passionate about combining agriculture with technology to support rural development and community empowerment. Being from Nepal, I have a deep connection with agriculture and a strong desire to contribute to its advancement through innovation.

I’m especially interested in digital solutions that improve access to agricultural knowledge and market opportunities. Outside of academics, I enjoy some blogging about current affairs, contemporary society, agriculture technology, education, and financial education.

I also have experience in such graphic design, basic website development, and web design. I aspire to create meaningful digital tools that connect knowledge, people, and progress in the agricultural sector.
