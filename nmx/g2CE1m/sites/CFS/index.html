About our Family

Chand Families Society (चन्द पारिवारिक समाज) is established to undertake the works relating to our associated family members by streamlining several social, cultural, economical and well being development of families etc. activities. To bring solidarity, it was felt necessary to establish this organisation.

Welcome to Our family society webpage which provides. us with a wide range of information about our community, It was founded with the aim of the promotion of the religious, social, educational, and cultural exchange between the people of our community. Its main goals are to unite, preserve, protect and promote social, economic and educational contacts of Samaj Members Residing. These goals would be best achieved by bringing everyone together, by establishing communication and bonding. This is the reason, here we can communicate with our people more efficiently.

Our Mission

A Friendly society where every individual from each segment becomes economically strong and realizes their potential. We contribute to building a society free from all forms of human deprivation, where equity prevails and no one is exploited, hungry, unemployed, financially weak, or illiterate. Our mission is to save lives, enable social protection, and defeat poverty.
